# Olist Business KPIs

## Overview

This document contains key business performance metrics used in the
Olist supply-chain analysis. These metrics provide a high-level view
of revenue, order activity, product performance, customer satisfaction,
and delivery performance.

---

## Monthly Revenue

### Definition

Monthly revenue represents the total order value generated during
each calendar month.

### Calculation

Revenue is aggregated from `total_price` by month using the
`order_purchase_timestamp`.

### Business Use

Monthly revenue is used to identify changes in overall business
performance and revenue trends over time.

---

## Monthly Orders

### Definition

Monthly orders represent the number of unique orders placed during
each calendar month.

### Calculation

Orders are aggregated by month using the unique `order_id` count.

### Business Use

Monthly order volume provides an indication of customer purchasing
activity and overall demand.

---

## Product Category Revenue

### Definition

Product category revenue represents the total revenue generated by
each product category.

### Analysis

Product categories are aggregated by month and ranked according to
total revenue. The analysis focuses on the top five revenue-generating
categories.

### Business Use

Category-level revenue analysis helps identify the product categories
that contribute most to overall revenue.

---

## Customer Review Score

### Definition

Customer review score represents the average customer review rating.

### Calculation

The mean review score is calculated using `mean_review_score`.

### Business Use

Customer review score is used as an indicator of customer satisfaction
and is also used in the sentiment analysis performed in this project.

---

## Delivery Performance

### Definition

Delivery performance is evaluated using delivery duration and the
late-delivery indicator.

### Key Metrics

- `delivery_days`: Total delivery duration.
- `is_late`: Indicator showing whether an order was delivered late.
- `carrier_transit_days`: Time spent in carrier transit.
- `vendor_handling_days`: Time spent in vendor handling.

### Business Use

These metrics are used to evaluate logistics performance and examine
the relationship between delivery performance and customer satisfaction.

---

## Business KPI Relationships

The project analyses business KPIs together with logistics and
customer metrics rather than treating them independently.

Examples include:

- Revenue and monthly order volume.
- Delivery performance and customer review scores.
- Logistics performance and SLA breaches.
- Customer segments and purchasing behaviour.
- Delivery performance and sentiment.

These relationships provide the foundation for the project's
business intelligence and conversational analytics components.
